# Types of User


merSETA Leadership & Planning Teams: Executive members and staff responsible for strategic research and sector skills planning within the MER sector.1

Employers & Employer Bodies: Organizations, particularly in the manufacturing and engineering sectors, that utilize the platform to understand talent pipelines and workforce needs.1

Education & Training Providers: TVETs, universities, and private providers who use the platform for curriculum alignment and to ensure training offerings meet current labor market demand.12

Government Administrators: Departments and agencies, such as the Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET), that rely on the data for national-scale strategic planning and institutional mechanism development.12

Industry Experts & Researchers: Professionals who utilize the platform’s analytical insights to study trends, validate findings, and provide expert evidence for policy interventions.2

Professional Bodies & Certification Partners: Entities involved in setting standards and certifying competencies, particularly those relevant to specific MER sub-sectors.1

Learners & Job Seekers: Individuals who use the platform for career guidance, identifying high-demand roles, and finding relevant learning pathways.13

1. **General User**

A user that has read-only access to the platform. This is any user from the general public who wants to use the platform to find information about skills and qualifications.


2. **Expert User**

An expert user has more than read-only access to the platform. This user has access to also contribute to the platform by sharing their expertise and opinion in a community of other experts in the domain. Expert users include:
- Industry Experts & Researchers
- Professional Bodies & Certification Partners
- Education & Training Providers
- merSETA Leadership & Planning Teams
- Government Administrators
- Employer & Employer Bodies


3. **Admin User**

An admin user is a user that has administrative capabilities
