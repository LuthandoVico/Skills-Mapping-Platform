# Skills Mapping Platform

## Vision

The Skills Mapping Platform is an AI-powered labour market intelligence system that assists job seekers in understanding current and future career opportunities.

The platform combines expert knowledge, labour market information, AI reasoning, and skill forecasting to provide career guidance.

---

## Personas

### Job Seeker

- Search occupations
- View career information
- View future demand
- View education pathways
- Upload CV (Phase 2)
- Receive AI recommendations

### Expert

- Validate occupation information
- Edit occupation descriptions
- Annotate specific sections
- Comment on changes
- Participate in expert discussions

### Administrator

- Approve experts
- Manage users
- Approve occupation changes
- Moderate discussions
